Caching means keeping a copy of something close by so you do not have to fetch it
again. On the web this is why a site you have visited before loads faster than one
you have not.

The trade-off is the only thing worth remembering at this level: a copy that is
kept too long shows people something out of date, and a copy that is thrown away
too quickly costs the speed you were trying to buy.
