Students design and run an investigation into what limits the rate of
photosynthesis, using pondweed and a lamp at measured distances.

They choose the variable, justify the control, and account for the fact that the
lamp heats the water as well as lighting it — which is the confound most groups
miss on the first attempt and the reason this is a practice-depth task rather
than a walkthrough.
