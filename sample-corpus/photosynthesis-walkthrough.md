Work through where each input ends up.

Light hits chlorophyll inside the chloroplasts. That energy splits water, which
releases the oxygen the plant gives off and leaves hydrogen behind. The hydrogen
is then joined to carbon dioxide to build glucose.

The point students miss: the oxygen comes from the water, not from the carbon
dioxide. Ask them to trace the oxygen atoms before you give them the equation,
because once the equation is on the board they stop tracing and start balancing.
