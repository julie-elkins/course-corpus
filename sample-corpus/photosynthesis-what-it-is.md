Plants make their own food. They take in water through their roots and carbon
dioxide through their leaves, and they use energy from sunlight to turn those two
things into sugar. The sugar is the plant's food. Oxygen comes out as a leftover,
which is the part that matters to us.

That is the whole idea at this stage. No equation yet, no chloroplasts yet.
